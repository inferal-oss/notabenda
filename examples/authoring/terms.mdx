## A small place for ideas

Juniper Notes is a fictional space for collecting thoughtful ideas. These example terms demonstrate **MDX authoring**, rather than a contract ready for a real service.

<PolicyNote title="Before you begin">
  Please use the sample thoughtfully. Your own terms need to reflect your service and receive appropriate review.
</PolicyNote>

## Your words stay yours

You keep ownership of the words you bring. In this fictional example, we use them only to provide the space you asked for.

- Keep a copy of anything important.
- Respect other people's work.
- Read the [sample privacy notice](/authored/privacy).

## When things change

We keep a revision history so reviewers can see the exact source and the rendered result. A new source file creates a new immutable release.
