No agency endorsement is implied.

The source page cites older regulations. Current link requirements come from [CCPA regulations effective 1 January 2026, section 7015](https://cppa.ca.gov/regulations/pdf/ccpa_statute_eff_20260101.pdf), not the old citation. The 2026 rule permits color adjustment for visibility. The supplied inline rendering retains the source's default geometry and colors; operators still review prominence, peer-icon size, background and placement.
